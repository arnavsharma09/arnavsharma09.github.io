Research Focus

I am an undergraduate at IIT Jodhpur (B.Tech, 2024–2028) building empirical evaluation frameworks for LLM agent security, deterministic runtime guardrails, and programmatically verified data-science systems.

My recent work investigates how tool-augmented language models fail under adaptive indirect prompt injection (using multi-armed bandit search over compositional attack grammars) and how argument-provenance taint tracking and AST-level static sandboxing can enforce security invariants without relying on stochastic LLM judges.

Across statistical and tabular systems, I focus on structural assumption verification—building multi-agent pipelines in Polars and LangGraph that enforce distributional checks (Shapiro-Wilk normality gating, non-parametric fallbacks, and DeepEval faithfulness assertions) prior to output synthesis.
